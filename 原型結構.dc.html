材料的起點

在每一個結構的誕生之前，材料即已存在於自然之中。它們不是被動的元素，而是潛藏著形態與力學的語言。AS Studio 認為，材料的選擇並非單純的建築手段，而是重新理解自然邏輯的過程。

The Origin of Materials

Before any structure is conceived, materials already dwell in nature. They are not passive elements but carriers of form and force. For AS Studio, the choice of material is not merely a technical decision—it is a process of rediscovering the logic of nature.

原型的展現

每一種材料皆蘊含一種「原型」：木的延展、石的重量、鋼的張力、竹的韌性。這些特質構成了建築的語法，也啟發了新的結構思維。原型不是形式的模仿，而是從材料中被感知出的法則，透過實驗與建構，轉化為可被居住的秩序。

The Manifestation of Prototypes

Each material embodies a "prototype": the elasticity of wood, the gravity of stone, the tension of steel, the resilience of bamboo. These qualities form the syntax of architecture and inspire new structural thinking. A prototype is not an imitation of form, but a sensed law within material—transformed through experiment and construction into a habitable order.

構築的共鳴

當材料被組構為結構，它不再只是物質的集合，而是時間、力量與工藝的交織。AS Studio 在設計中追求的不僅是穩定性與美感，更是材料之間的共鳴——一種讓空間得以「呼吸」的結構節奏，讓建築回應土地與環境的聲音。

Resonance in Construction

When materials are composed into structure, they cease to be a mere aggregation of matter—they become an intersection of time, force, and craft. In its designs, AS Studio pursues not only stability and beauty but resonance among materials: a structural rhythm that allows space to breathe and architecture to echo the voice of the land.
